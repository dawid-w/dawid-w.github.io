Frequently asked

Is there a limit on conversations?
There is a fair-use allowance rather than a fixed number — how far it goes depends on how long and complex your requests are. Premium raises it considerably.
What happens when I reach it?
Tasks, calendar and notes keep working exactly as before. Only the conversation pauses, and it resets shortly after.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, one tap in settings. Premium runs until the end of the paid period.
